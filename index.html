📖 How to play

Grab guns in buildings, medkits (+45 HP), blue shields (20s no damage), the orange meteor (map-wide shower, only a shield saves you), the lightning ring (touch it and you die) and lightning grenades. A flamethrower is hidden in the Fire Station. Cars and the train hurt you, so grab a medkit to heal. Zombie mode: it is dark, zombies bite, and rivals shoot. First to 25 kills wins.
PC: WASD move, mouse aim, click shoot, 1-6 / E swap gun, Q grenade.
Phone (landscape): D-pad on the left moves, FIRE on the right auto-aims at the nearest enemy. Drag on the right side to aim by hand.
